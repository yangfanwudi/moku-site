GFM tables and task lists just work, and clicking a task checkbox in the preview writes the change back into your source.

数据备份Backup

数据始终在你自己手里 Your data stays in your hands

墨库不上传任何数据。所有笔记存在浏览器 IndexedDB 里，用顶栏的「JSON / ZIP」按钮随时打包备份或迁移设备。 Moku uploads nothing. Notes live in your browser's IndexedDB — use the JSON / ZIP buttons in the top bar to back up or move devices at any time.

1JSON 快照JSON snapshot

一次导出全部笔记与元数据（含导出时间），恢复时完整还原，适合整库搬迁。 Export every note plus metadata (with the export time) in one file, and restore it wholesale — ideal for moving the whole library.

2ZIP 打包ZIP bundle

每篇笔记一个独立 .md（slug 文件名 + YAML front-matter），附 manifest.json 清单，可直接归档或在其他工具里继续用。 One standalone .md per note (slug filename + YAML front-matter) plus a manifest.json — ready for your archive or any other tool.

3导入：合并或覆盖Import: merge or overwrite

校验结构后可选「增量合并」（id 冲突时保留 updated_at 较新者）或「全量覆盖」（单事务原子写入）。 After validating the file, choose an incremental merge (the newer updated_at wins on id conflicts) or a full overwrite in one atomic transaction.

⚠️

清空浏览器站点数据会连同笔记一起清掉——换设备、重装系统前，请先用顶栏 JSON / ZIP 按钮导出备份。 Clearing the browser's site data takes your notes with it — before switching devices or reinstalling, export a backup with the JSON / ZIP buttons.

常见问题FAQ

你可能想问 Good questions

我的数据保存在哪里？会上传到云端吗？Where is my data stored? Does it upload anywhere?

全部保存在浏览器本地的 IndexedDB（库名 md-knowledge-base），墨库没有任何账号体系或云端同步，数据不出你的浏览器。

Everything is stored in your browser's local IndexedDB (database md-knowledge-base). Moku has no accounts and no cloud sync — your data never leaves your browser.

说是离线应用，为什么首次打开还需要联网？It's an offline app — why does the first open need internet?

应用本体是单文件、零本地资源请求，双击就能打开；但 Vue 3、Tailwind CSS、marked、highlight.js、KaTeX、JSZip 这些依赖库通过 CDN 引入，首次打开需要联网加载，之后浏览器会缓存它们。

The app itself is a single file that requests no local resources and opens on double-click — but its dependency libraries (Vue 3, Tailwind CSS, marked, highlight.js, KaTeX, JSZip) are loaded from a CDN. The first open needs internet; after that, the browser caches them.

怎么把笔记迁移到新电脑？How do I move my notes to a new computer?

在旧设备点顶栏「JSON」导出快照，再到新设备打开墨库导入：可选「增量合并」（按 id 冲突时保留更新时间较新者）或「全量覆盖」。偏好通用格式的话，「ZIP」会把每篇笔记导出成独立 .md 文件。

Export a JSON snapshot from the old device's top bar, then import it on the new one — choose an incremental merge (newer timestamp wins on conflicts) or a full overwrite. Prefer portable formats? The ZIP export gives you one standalone .md per note.

支持哪些浏览器？Which browsers are supported?

推荐使用 Chrome / Edge 打开——墨库的存储（IndexedDB）、拖拽（HTML5 Drag & Drop）与渲染均基于现代浏览器标准能力。

Chrome / Edge are the recommended browsers — Moku's storage (IndexedDB), dragging (HTML5 Drag & Drop) and rendering all rely on standard modern-browser capabilities.

「编辑」和「看板」两种模式怎么切换？How do I switch between Edit and Board modes?

顶栏有模式切换：编辑模式是左源码右预览的双栏；看板模式把笔记按「待办 / 进行中 / 已完成」三泳道排开，点击任意卡片即可回到该笔记的编辑模式。

Use the mode switch in the top bar: Edit mode is the source-plus-preview split pane; Board mode lays your notes out across To-Do / In Progress / Done lanes, and clicking any card takes you back to that note in the editor.

把墨库装进 U 盘 Put Moku on a USB stick

一个 ZIP 压缩包，装着应用本体 index.html 与 README.md 说明文档。解压到桌面、U 盘或网盘的任意文件夹，双击即用。 One ZIP holding the app itself (index.html) and the README.md docs. Unzip it anywhere — desktop, USB stick or cloud drive — and double-click to run.
